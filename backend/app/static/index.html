How the RL Policy Splits Each Depot's Fuel
–

Every tick the PPO policy reads each station's stock, 4-hour demand forecast, incoming deliveries, stockout risk, demand spikes and route status. For every station and fuel it outputs a priority (1–5, how costly a shortfall there is) and a safety margin (extra buffer on top of forecast demand). The need is the litres required to cover the next hours with that margin. A linear program then sends each depot's fuel to the reachable stations, serving higher-priority stations first, within route, truck and dispatch limits. Recomputed live on the current state.

Recommended Shipments · best plan right now
–

Every solver (all 7 including PPO; fewer in budget mode when the system is under load) plans on the current state; each plan is simulated on the digital twin under low, expected and high demand, and the best score wins. This is the plan the system recommends. Nothing is shipped from this view: it runs when the decision cycle executes it, or when you press ⚡ Decide Now.
